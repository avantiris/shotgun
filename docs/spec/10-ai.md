# 10 AI on the picture (`PROPOSED` throughout)

Asked for on 2026-11-22 (feedback N2.5): "a potential AI integration for
the video editor so that I could use AI to enhance the video (e.g. change
background etc) ... if possible it should be a local vendored model but
if that doesnt work ... then just use an API optional". Nothing here was
agreed with the user: it is what was built and planned in that night.

## What exists: "Cut out the person (AI)"

- In the menu of a video asset. It makes a new asset, "<name> cut out":
  the same video with everything but the person transparent. Put on a
  track above a colour, a picture or another clip, that is a changed
  background; the original stays as it is.
- A clip of the video that is already on the timeline is switched to
  the cut-out from its menu, "Show only the person (the cut-out)", in
  the same place and time (and back with "Show the whole picture
  again"); its sound stays the video's. What lies on the tracks below is
  then the background.
- "A copy out of focus" in the same menu makes a blurred copy of the
  video (no model: a wide blur by ffmpeg). Under the cut-out, at the
  same time, it gives the look of a lens with its background out of
  focus. The blurred copy still has the person in it, so a soft shadow
  of the person shows around the sharp one.
- Local: the model (Robust Video Matting, MobileNetV3, 15 MB) runs on
  the CPU in a helper program (`sg-matte`: Python with onnxruntime,
  packaged with the program). Nothing is sent anywhere. The model file
  is fetched once from its authors' release page into
  `<library>/_models/` and checked against a known SHA-256 before use.
- It is a background job like an import: progress among the import jobs,
  stopped with the job's cancel button there, one run per asset at a time.
- Measured (`docs/spikes/s8-matte.md`): about 4 frames a second at 1080p
  on this machine while it was busy, so about fifteen times the length of
  a 1080p60 clip; 77 MB of cache per second of 1080p60 for the lossless
  copy that transparent video is played from.
- Made for one person in front of a camera. Not for objects, not for
  crowds. Hair and fast hands come out soft.
- Licence: the model's code is GPL-3.0. That matters only if Shotgun is
  ever given to others.

Not built: the sound of the original in the cut-out (place the original's sound
under it); edge controls (shrink, feather); a choice of model.

## What exists: "Remove the sky (AI)" (2026-11-22, feedback F17.2, F17.4)

- In the menu of a clip of a video on the timeline: "Remove the sky
  (AI)". The sky becomes transparent (as the cut-out's background is):
  what lies on the tracks below shows there, a sky from footage or a
  picture of one's own.
- Only the part of the video that the clip shows is made (so: cut the
  shot first). It arrives as an asset of its own ("<video> clear sky
  <from>-<length>"), and the clip then shows it, in the same place and
  time; its sound stays the video's. "The picture as it was" in the
  clip's menu, or undo, takes it back. The same part is made once.
- How (`docs/spikes/s9-sky.md`): a model marks the sky (skyseg, MIT
  licence, 176 MB, fetched once into `<library>/_models/` and checked),
  and the mark is fitted to each frame's edges (`sg-sky`: Python with
  onnxruntime and OpenCV, packaged with the program). Local; nothing is
  sent anywhere.
- A background job among the import jobs, with progress and cancel; one
  at a time, further ones wait.
- About 4 frames a second at 1080p: a minute for four seconds of 60
  frames a second.
- Known: a plain bright wall can be taken for sky.
- The three skies that Shotgun drew itself in their place (storm,
  sunset, night) were taken out again on the same day (F17.4, agreed):
  what he wanted was a model that paints the sky into the whole
  picture, which is the next section.

## What exists: "Change by a sentence (AI)..." (2026-11-22, feedback F17.4)

Agreed in F17.4: the whole picture goes to a generative model, which may
change more than the sky; the integration is not tied to one service;
a local model is tried as a spike (`docs/spikes/s10-local-video-edit.md`).
The rest of this section is `PROPOSED`.

- In the menu of a clip of a video on the timeline. A window asks for
  the model and the sentence ("Dark, epic storm clouds in the sky"; four
  sentences to begin from).
- Before anything is sent the window says what leaves the machine (the
  part of the picture the clip shows, without sound, at most 1920
  points on its longer side, and the sentence), where it goes, and what
  it costs as far as the file says. Nothing is ever sent without that
  click; never by itself, never in an export.
- What comes back is fitted to the part (its size, frame rate and exact
  number of frames; a shorter answer holds its last picture), arrives
  as an asset ("<video> changed <8 letters> <from>-<length>") and takes
  the clip's place like a part without sky does, with the same way
  back. The timeline never depends on the service afterwards.
- Which models there are is written in `~/.config/shotgun/ai.toml`
  (`SHOTGUN_AI` names another file), outside every project, read each
  time the window opens. With no file the window shows an example.
  One `[[service]]` per model:
  - `name`, `kind`: `fal`, `runway`, `replicate` or `command`.
  - `model`: the service's name for it (Replicate also
    `owner/name:version`). `key` or `key_env`.
  - `price_per_second`, `min_seconds`, `max_seconds`: for the estimate
    and to refuse a part the model does not take. Written by the user.
  - `video_field`, `prompt_field`, `[service.input]`: the names of the
    request's fields where a model has other ones, and whatever else it
    takes. `prompt = "... {sentence} ..."` puts words around the
    sentence. `result`: where in the answer the video's address is.
    With these, any video-to-video model of the three services is used
    without a change to the program.
  - `kind = "command"`: `command = ["program", "{in}", "{out}",
    "{prompt}"]`, any program on this machine: a local model, or a
    script for a service that is none of the three. A line `progress
    0.4` on its output is shown; a key reaches it as `SHOTGUN_AI_KEY`.
- The code is `crates/sg-project/src/ai.rs`. The three services are
  spoken to as their documents said on 2026-11-22 (read by an agent,
  not all pages verified; sources in the feedback item). **None of the
  three has been tried with a real key**: the tests run them against a
  stand-in on this machine. The first real use is the test.
- Jobs at services run side by side; those on this machine one at a time.

## What should come next, in this order

1. A GPU path for the cut-out (ncnn on Vulkan is the likely one), and the
   matte kept beside the asset as a grey video that the renderer uses as
   transparency, instead of a second asset: less space, edge controls, and
   "blur the background" as one switch.
2. Slow motion by making frames in between (RIFE, on Vulkan; `video2x`
   in nixpkgs bundles it): a processed copy per asset, as stabilising is.
3. Upscaling small pictures (Real-ESRGAN, on Vulkan, in nixpkgs).
4. Depth from the picture, for a blurred background without cutting out.

## What stays planned for services

- Reference pictures with the sentence ("this sky"), where a model takes them.
- Looking prices up instead of having them written in the file.
- Sound: the part is sent without it, and what comes back has none.
