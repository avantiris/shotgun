### F3.3 Mixer and noise reduction are missing
- Said: "then there are obviously many things missing like mixer and capability to turn down the noise of e.g. an audio track etc"
- Kind: missing
- Area: audio
- Status: fixed (commit after this file); see 06-audio.md, last section
- Now: M or the Mixer button shows a strip per audio track (meter, fader,
  pan, mute, solo, low cut, noise reduction) and a master fader. It is in
  the export too.
- Measured on a test recording (voice-like tone with hiss, exported with and
  without): hiss alone -35.7 dB -> -78.5 dB with noise reduction at 100 %
  and a 100 Hz low cut; the voice lost about 0.8 dB beyond the fader change.
- Not verified: how it sounds (nothing was played through the speakers),
  the faders with a real mouse.
- "many things missing ... etc": EQ, compressor and limiter are not built;
  listed as open in 06-audio.md.
