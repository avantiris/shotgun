# Feedback round 6, 2026-11-21 (first use of the piano roll and of Kontakt)

### F16.1 The note editor scrolls and zooms like the timeline
- Said: "the note editor must also be scrollable / zoomable like the bottom timeline"
- Kind: workflow
- Area: other
- Status: fixed (15769e7)
- Now: the roll goes on after the clip's end (a bar of it is in view from the start, sixteen bars at a time, as far as the track is free), and a note written or pasted there makes the clip longer. Wheel: up and down; Shift + wheel: along the time; Ctrl + wheel: zoom; new: Alt + wheel for the rows' height and the middle button to drag the view. The wheel was not changed to move along the time as on the timeline: up and down the keys is needed more often in a roll (to be confirmed by him).
- Verified: a headless flow, a screenshot. Not verified by a person.

### F16.2 Right click does not always remove a note
- Said: "Then sometimes right click to remove notes does not properly work"
- Kind: bug
- Area: other
- Status: fixed (15769e7)
- Cause: the removal waited for a "click"; a hand that moved a little while pressing made it a drag, and nothing happened.
- Now: the note goes when the right button goes down, and every note it is then drawn over; one undo step.
- Verified: a headless flow with the pointer moving while the button is held.

### F16.5 Instruments other than Kontakt
- Said: "also a way to add instruments that are not kontakt 8 but other vst3 plugins (but maybe that works if i add a track and have another vst loaded)"
- Kind: missing
- Area: audio
- Status: exists
- Note: "+ Track" lists every plugin found (VST3 and CLAP in ~/.vst3, ~/.clap, the system's folders and VST3_PATH / CLAP_PATH; Windows VST3 in the Wine prefix after "Find the installed plugins"), and "Add plugin" on a strip does the same for an existing track. Checked with Surge XT (the test plugin) and Kontakt 8 only.

### F16.6 The pointer and where a click lands are apart in the piano roll
- Said: "still th e offset of my cursor and where I click in teh music panel is not really properly done right now..."
- Kind: bug
- Area: other
- Status: fixed (9523b96)
- Cause: found by reading the code, not by reproducing his clicks: once the roll was scrolled up or down (and often from the start), the lower part of every row counted as the note below, by up to a whole row.
- Now: a point anywhere in a row is that row's pitch.
- Verified: a unit test over scrolled positions and row heights. Not verified by a person; if an offset is left along the time, it is another cause.
